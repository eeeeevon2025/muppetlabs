IA Move · Friction resolution

Two doors become one.

Today, admins can reach the same list of AI automations from two different rail destinations: the AI for Customers selector dropdown and the Manage Automations page. Both render variations of the same list with different controls. This proposal merges them into a single Automations destination with an inline switcher pattern.

03How it works in practice

Five behaviors that fall out of merging the two destinations.

1

One door, one URL

The rail item routes to /ai/automations. Clicking an automation row deep-links to /ai/automations/{id}. URLs are shareable and back-button works.

2

List controls vs detail controls

Add, filter, sort, bulk select live in the toolbar above the list. Edit, deploy, duplicate, delete live on the selected automation's tabs and overflow menu. No duplicated controls.

3

AI Setup lands you here

The wizard's final step adds the new automation to this list and selects it. The admin sees their new automation as a row alongside the rest, with full context.

4

Switcher = the list itself

No dropdown switcher in the rail. Switching between automations is clicking another row. The list IS the switcher — there's only one mental model.

5

Suggestions surface here too

When the Validation Agent drafts a fix for an automation, the suggestion card lands on its row with the inline slide-out edit pattern. Same surface, no jump-back-to-Analyze.

6

The IA simplifies

One rail item under AI replaces two. The "AIC selector vs Manage Automations" friction row in the IA recommendation collapses entirely.
