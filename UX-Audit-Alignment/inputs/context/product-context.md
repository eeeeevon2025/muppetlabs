## What the product does

Fans answer twelve questions to get matched with one of fourteen Muppet personality archetypes. The site now includes **Compare with a Friend** (duo quiz + compatibility verdict) and **Build Your Muppet Show** (5-step cast wizard + shareable episode card), plus cast directory, theory, trivia, leaderboard, news, demo store, and contact.

## Primary jobs to be done

1. **Discover my Muppet match** — Quiz → result → share
2. **Compare with someone else** — Invite link → friend quiz → compatibility reveal
3. **Build a dream variety show** — Wizard → episode card → share
4. **Understand the personality model** — Theory + cast profiles
5. **Engage with community content** — News, trivia, leaderboard
