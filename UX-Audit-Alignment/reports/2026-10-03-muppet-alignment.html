Prototype alignment audit

The quiz is the job.
The rail disagrees.

The product context names one primary surface: quiz, then result. The built site puts that quiz in a rail of eleven equal destinations, including a demo store, trivia, news, and a show builder that does not need a match.

01Codified principles

Rules the built site is already following. Each one can be checked against the tree and the journey.

  1. The quiz is the front door. The home hero button is “Take the quiz,” and the page closes on “Start the quiz.”
  2. Compare borrows the quiz. An invite opens /quiz?compare=, keeps the same twelve questions, and only changes the badge to “Friend compare” and the title to “Take the quiz to compare.”
  3. The result is the handoff. “Copy share link,” “Compare with a friend,” and “Build your Muppet show” all sit on the result, not in the rail.
  4. The directory holds the cast. “Read the full profile” goes to a /muppets/[slug] page. Home’s “See full directory” goes to “Muppet Directory.”
  5. Theory is a preface, not a gate. The secondary hero action is “Read the theory first.” The quiz never requires Theory.
  6. A show is five steps, then a card. Show builder steps are Show details, Director, Main cast, Conflict, and Finale. Submit publishes an episode card.
  7. An invite is not a shared session. “Compare with a friend” on the result creates a token. The friend takes the quiz separately.
  8. The rail does not rank jobs. Quiz, Compare, Show, Muppets, Theory, News, Leaderboard, Trivia, Store, Contact, and About render as the same kind of link.
  9. A name is optional. The last quiz step says “What should we call you on your result? Totally optional.” The field placeholder is “Anonymous Fan.”
  10. A result does not require an account. Home says “No email required to see your result.” The product context says storage is file-based JSON, with no user accounts.

03End-to-end journey

Alex wants a match, then wants a friend to take the same quiz. The result page is where that loop can close. The rail offers several ways to leave before it does.

1

Land on the pitch

Home

Alex reads “Which Muppet are you, actually?” and the line “Answer twelve questions.” The accent button is “Take the quiz.” Beside it, “Read the theory first” is available and not required.

2

Answer the curtain question

Home → Quiz

The title is “The Muppet Personality Quiz.” Question one asks, “Backstage, five minutes before curtain, something is on fire (again). You:” Alex chooses “Add it to the show — the audience will love it,” then clicks Next. Eleven questions remain. Back is available after the first answer.

3

Name the result, optionally

Home → Quiz

The last step is titled “One last thing” and says “What should we call you on your result? Totally optional.” Alex types Alex. The button reads “See my result,” then “Matching you…” while the result is saved.

4

Read the match

Home → Quiz → Result

The page says “You are…” and the name of one of the fourteen cast members, plus trait bars and a runner-up when one exists. This audit does not claim which cast member Alex matched. That depends on the twelve answers.

Copy share link

The button label flips to “Link copied!” The result URL can be sent as-is. No friend quiz is started.

Compare with a friend

The button creates an invite and opens Compare invite. This is the only control that starts a compare.

Leave the match

“Read the full profile,” “Take it again,” and “Build your Muppet show” are also on this page. The show path does not use the match.

→ Alex chooses Compare with a friend
5

Wait for the friend

Result → Compare invite

Before the friend finishes, the page title is “Waiting for friend.” The friend is sent to the quiz with the compare token. If the invite expires, the page says “This compare link timed out” and offers “Take the quiz” and “About compare.”

6

Friend takes the same quiz

Compare invite → Quiz

The friend’s quiz badge is “Friend compare” and the title is “Take the quiz to compare.” The questions are the same Quiz set. There is no second questionnaire.

7

The verdict closes the loop

Quiz → Compare invite

The page title becomes “Compare results.” The heading is one of five verdicts from the compare model: “Same Muppet, same energy,” “Near twins backstage,” “Opposites, on purpose,” “The classic duo,” or “Different icons, shared stage.” Two result cards, a trait-overlap panel, and “Map distance” sit under it. The actions are “Copy compare link,” “Take the quiz,” and “Build your Muppet show.”

04Match, then hand off

This section follows the orchestrator and the report architecture. The detailed pattern-extraction file was not in the source pack.

The structural loop is not a recommendation engine. It is: a signal on Home, twelve answers, a named result, then a handoff the result page has to offer because the rail will not.

Stage 1, Signal

  • Home states the job in the headline and in “Answer twelve questions.”
  • The same page also offers Compare, Show, Theory, Trivia, Store, and Leaderboard before the quiz starts.
From the journey: Alex can click “Take the quiz” or leave through “Open show builder,” which skips the explainer and opens Show builder.

Stage 2, Answer

  • QuizWizard asks twelve questions. Next and Back move through them.
  • Compare does not add questions. It sets ?compare= on the same wizard.
From the journey: “Add it to the show — the audience will love it” is a real option on question one, not a paraphrase.

Stage 3, Reveal

  • Result says “You are…” plus a cast name, trait bars, and a runner-up link into Profile.
  • The page is the first place the match can be shared, compared, or abandoned for the show builder.
From the journey: “See my result” is the commit. “Copy share link” does not create an invite.

Stage 4, Hand off

  • “Compare with a friend” creates the invite and opens Compare invite.
  • The loop closes when Compare results shows a verdict headline, two cards, and a map distance.
The four places a finished match can go
Copy share link — copies the result URL. No second person enters the quiz.
Compare with a friend — creates the token and waits on Compare invite.
Read the full profile — opens that cast member’s Profile.
Build your Muppet show — opens Show builder with no requirement to use the match.

05Risks

This section follows the orchestrator and the report architecture. The detailed risk-rubric file was not in the source pack. Severity here is the blast radius stated in the row, not a separate score.

#RiskSeverityWhy it hurts
1 Eleven equal rail items High NavLinks gives Store, News, Trivia, and Leaderboard the same treatment as Quiz. A first visit can miss “Discover my Muppet match,” the primary job in the product context. Blast radius: the whole first screen.
2 Compare in the rail cannot start a compare Medium Nav “Compare” opens an explainer whose button is “Take the quiz first.” The invite is created only by “Compare with a friend” on the result. Blast radius: anyone who starts from the rail item named Compare.
3 Show builder ignores the match Medium Home links “Open show builder” straight to Show builder. The five steps never require a quiz result, so the episode card can be unrelated to “which Muppet are you.” Blast radius: the show job, and the result-page handoff that offers it anyway.
4 No baseline to price the drift Low The product context says the primary surface is Quiz → Result, and the repo contains the expanded site. There is no older snapshot, so this audit cannot say what was added later or what a migration would cost. Blast radius: the confidence of every “new” claim. None are marked new.

06Friction and resolutions

Places the same job has two doors, or a door that looks like the job and is not.

FrictionWhat’s happeningContract
Compare has two doors The rail opens Compare, an explainer that sends Alex to Quiz. The result opens Compare invite by creating a token. Only the second one starts the friend loop. The result button is the only invite creator. The rail page is instructions. That split is already true in the code. It is not explained on the explainer.
Show has two doors Nav “Show” opens the five-step explainer. Home’s “Open show builder” skips it and opens Show builder. “Start building” on the explainer also goes to Show builder. Show is the explainer. Show builder is the wizard. Home currently treats the wizard as the front door.
The verdict does not return to the profiles Compare results shows two cast names and then “Copy compare link,” “Take the quiz,” and “Build your Muppet show.” “Read the full profile” exists only on Result. No contract in the IA yet. Stated intention, not a shipped rule: each verdict card should reach that cast member’s Profile.
A second quiz Resolved Compare could have shipped its own questions. It does not. The friend path reuses QuizWizard and changes the badge and title. Friend compare is /quiz?compare=. One question set. This is already reflected in Quiz and in the journey.

07Usability test plan

Hypothesis-driven, not yet field-validated. The product context lists jobs and does not cite a research study. No prior usability report is in this repo.

Match

Tests “The quiz is the front door” and the eleven-item rail.

1

Find the quiz from the home page

“You want to find out which Muppet you are. Start from the home page and get as far as the first question.”

Success The participant clicks “Take the quiz” or “Start the quiz” and reaches “The Muppet Personality Quiz” without opening Store, News, or Trivia.

Tests Principle “The quiz is the front door,” and risk 1.

2

Finish without inventing an account

“Answer the questions however you like. When it asks what to call you, decide whether to type a name. Get to the page that tells you who you are.”

Success The participant reaches Result. If they skip the name, the result still loads. They do not look for a sign-up form.

Tests “A name is optional” and “A result does not require an account.”

3

Tell the result from the directory

“You’re on the page that says which Muppet you are. Open the full write-up of that character, then come back.”

Success The participant uses “Read the full profile,” lands on that Profile, and can say it is the cast page rather than their personal result.

Tests “The directory holds the cast.”

Compare

Tests the two Compare doors and the reused quiz.

4

Start a compare from the rail

“You have not taken the quiz. A friend wants to compare results. Use the site navigation, not the home-page cards, and try to start that.”

Success The participant opens Compare, hits “Take the quiz first,” and can say they do not yet have an invite link.

Tests Friction “Compare has two doors,” and risk 2.

5

Start a compare from the result

“You already have a result. Send your friend something they can use to take the quiz against you.”

Success The participant clicks “Compare with a friend,” lands on Compare invite, and does not click “Copy share link” first. If they do, they notice it did not create the invite and recover without help.

Tests “An invite is not a shared session,” and the handoff targets in section 04.

6

Recognize the friend’s quiz

“Your friend opens the invite. Before answering, say what this quiz is for.”

Success The participant reads “Friend compare” or “Take the quiz to compare” and does not describe it as a different quiz.

Tests “Compare borrows the quiz,” and the resolved second-quiz row.

Show

Tests whether the episode card is understood as optional, and separate from the match.

7

Reach the builder from Home

“You want to cast a short Muppet show. Start on the home page. Get to the step that asks for a director.”

Success The participant uses “Open show builder” or the Show rail plus “Start building,” and reaches the Director step. Note which door they used.

Tests Friction “Show has two doors.”

8

Notice the quiz is not required

“You have not taken the personality quiz. Build a show anyway, using any cast members, and publish the episode card.”

Success The participant completes Show details, Director, Main cast, Conflict, and Finale and reaches an episode card without being sent to Quiz.

Tests Risk 3, “Show builder ignores the match.”

9

Leave the verdict without losing the match

“You’re looking at a finished compare. You want the full profile of one of the two Muppets, not a new quiz and not a show.”

Success Watch the path. The shipped page does not offer “Read the full profile” here. Record whether the participant hunts, gives up, or leaves through “Take the quiz.”

Tests Friction “The verdict does not return to the profiles.”
